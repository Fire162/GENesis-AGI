- **Genesis can pay with a vault card after your Telegram tap (off by default).** With `card_fill.enabled: true` in `config/payments.yaml` (and the browser restarted), the new `browser_fill_card` tool reopens the checkout in a separate, short-lived browser window that no other browser tool can reach and that shows blank on the display, fills the merchant's other fields (email, address and similar), asks you to approve with a tap that shows the site, amount, card, Pay button label and the names of the fields it fills, then fills the card, clicks Pay itself and closes the window. Genesis only ever sees a fixed status: after Pay it is `submitted_unverified`, or `challenge` / `outcome_unknown` with "do NOT retry"; a bank (3DS) check is not shown yet, so it closes the window and reports the outcome as unknown. While card fill is on, Camoufox keeps no form history, autofill, password capture, browsing history or disk cache for any site.
